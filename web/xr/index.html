LDraw MR
In mixed reality
Both hands work the same, with the trigger (or a pinch). Point at a table or the floor to put the model there. Point at the model and hold to move it. Point at it and push that hand's thumbstick: up or down to scale it (from its base), left or right to turn it. The menu follows your view: Real size, Tabletop, Walk-in (minifig scale, on the floor), Stats, Exit. It closes once the model is in place; B or Y show or hide it. Elsewhere, the thumbsticks walk and turn you.
