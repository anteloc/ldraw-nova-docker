# Kestrel Mk IV - brief (total overhaul, floating pose, >600 parts)

Reference: Naganata (675 parts; black + dark-bluish-grey; ~36 cheese slopes, ~35 round plates, ~36 1x2 plates,
17 1x4 plates, Technic bricks + pins, side-stud skins, stepped layered plates). Counted from the MPD.

Silhouette: ~720 (length, -Z forward) x ~600 (span) x ~230 (to fin tip) LDU. Long needle nose that rises in
1-plate steps (each step ends in a row of cheese slopes), 4-stud wide fuselage with a canopy, a *window* in the hull
side that shows a pinned Technic spine (3701 pairs + 4459 pins), delta wings that step down toward a thin leading
edge, two tall engine nacelles on the wing roots (front intakes, rear nozzles on side-stud faces), swept brick fins.
Floating pose: no stand.

Palette (bold yellow kept per user): dark bluish grey 72 dominant, black 0 underside/panels, yellow 14 stripes
(flank, nacelle spine, wing leading edges, fin caps, cannon rings), trans-light-blue 43 thruster cores, trans-black canopy.

Construction: ship-frame plate/brick stacks generated by a seeded plate packer (alternating orientation per layer
so seams bond), keel L0/L1 spans the whole footprint so hull/wings/nacelles are bonded. SNOT skins (flank panels,
nacelle outer panels, intakes, nozzles, aft caps, wing cannons) are built upright in own sections and turned onto
30414 / 87087 side studs with origin = stud - R*socket (Halcyon technique).

Reuse: Halcyon example helpers (orient/seat, flank/nozzle/intake/cannon module recipes, cockpit interior, 30384 canopy
seating). Everything else (hull layout, packer, spine, nose, wings, nacelles, fins, details) is new.
Jev: not used (FTS / catalog only).
