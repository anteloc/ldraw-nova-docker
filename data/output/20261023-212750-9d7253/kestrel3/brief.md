# Kestrel Mk III - brief
Style source: Naganata (Technic 3703/3701 spine pinned with 4459, layered wing pairs, dish thrusters). Bold yellow stripes kept (user choice), but placed on structure.
Silhouette: long arrowhead ~720 x 560 x 150 LDU; low pointed nose rising to a canopy; two stacked swept wing pairs per side (long rear, shorter front); tall twin fins; three dish thrusters (trans-blue) at the rear; display stand through the spine.
Palette: dark bluish grey 72 dominant, black 0 breaks/underside, yellow 14 on leading edges / nacelle strips / cannon rings, trans-light-blue 43 thruster cores, trans-black canopy.
Construction: studs-up Technic spine (3703/3701 + 4459 pins, bare-core render) -> plate layers -> 30414 side-stud course -> SNOT skins built upright and turned onto stud faces (flanks, intakes, exhausts, cannons) -> wings on bonded plate splices -> canopy -> stand.
Reuse: Halcyon example generator helpers (orient/seat/Section) and skeleton ideas; new spine, wings, nose, fins, thrusters.
