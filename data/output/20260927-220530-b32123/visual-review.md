# Azure Sprint visual review

Opened first-render home, right, front, back, top and bottom PNGs. The narrow roof and exposed studded cabin looked more like an unfinished cabin than a racing accent. Revised the two recessed cabin-surround plates to dark bluish grey and added a white rear deck marker to repeat the bonnet stripes; did not add unsupported parts over the windows. Opened final home, top, right and bottom PNGs. The low, long bonnet, defined fenders, visible two-tone livery, black grille, clear headlamps and red tail lamps read consistently. The cabin remains deliberately open between two smoky windscreens, with visible studs; this is a display coupe, not a proven seated minifigure car. The bottom confirms a narrow two-axle System chassis; no wheel-space candidates were reported. Final front and back PNGs were generated but were opened on the first revision only; changes after that did not touch lamp or fascia geometry, only two colours on the upper body.

No visible missing wheel or major intersection in opened images. Wheel-pin retention, clutch strength, material intersection of curved arches, rolling freedom and real part/colour availability have not been established by rendering. Validation reports nine optimistic connector groups, including coverage limitations from nonrectangular parts; the road check passes, and the LeoCAD/Python inventories agree. Physical buildability remains not proven.
