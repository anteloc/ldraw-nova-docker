# Cobalt Courier — final visual review

Opened the first six views. The original tall brick parcel overwhelmed the bike; first revision changed it to a layered low pack. The published preview of that revision still looked too tall, so the generator was revised again to **one plate and one tile** on the rack. Rebuilt, rendered, and opened the final home, right, top, back, bottom and front PNGs. The revised pack sits beneath the handlebar silhouette; the fairing, exposed engine, fork and two spoked wheels are readable. Lamp faces forward and the rear tyre sits below a clear arched fender. Bottom view shows wheel alignment. The bike has only two tyres; without a stand or rider it is not self-standing.

Final geometry validation complete, checks passed, ten physical placements. Motorcycle check passed (107-LDU wheelbase, both tyres at Y=0, zero supported wheel-space candidates). LeoCAD import passed and Python/LeoCAD BOMs match 10/10. Two assembly warnings remain: eight optimistic connection groups because the molded frame lacks connector metadata and tyre/fairing/headlamp interfaces are under-detected; oriented bounding-box candidates for complex curved parts require actual material/clearance inspection. The parcel plate/rack and lid/plate each have two potential stud/socket contacts. Physical buildability, pin retention, lamp fit, retail colour availability and stability are not certified.
