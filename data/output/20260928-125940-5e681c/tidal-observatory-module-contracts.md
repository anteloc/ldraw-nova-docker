Main hall center (-120,80), tower center (280,100). Quay base area includes the front harbor; no wall ornament occupies the window-opening volume. Rear of both hall levels deliberately has broad open exhibit cutaways with corner piers and bonded upper lintels. Roofs and upper storey lift away for access; the model is a display cutaway and does not claim a complete minifigure circulation/stair system.

Reference 5770 is silhouette/lantern technique only. Copper Lane contributes general window-pane alignment and shelf technique, rebuilt here with new dimensions and composition. No extracted source assembly is included in the final model.

Dome design refinement: the inspected curved parts support a closed, tiered copper cap rather than a working dome shutter. Telescope/scientific displays are static; roof rotation and internal stairs are not represented. Exact module checks and final visual reviews are recorded in the final reports.
