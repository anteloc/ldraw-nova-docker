# The Tidal Observatory

Original architectural display model by OpenAI for this request.

## Identity and appeal
A turn-of-the-century coastal institute: marine charts downstairs, an astronomical reading room upstairs, a weather lantern on the taller east tower, and a patinated observatory dome above the west hall. The contrast of a rounded green dome, a thin warm lantern, and a long limestone arcade makes the silhouette distinctive. The building sits on a raised quay beside a narrow strip of tiled sea.

## Composition
Approximately 48 x 40 studs; main hall about 28 x 20 studs with two furnished levels, and an offset narrow tower rising one additional level. Front is -Z. A projecting central entrance and broad stone stairs lead from the waterfront terrace. The rear has deliberate cutaway openings for seeing the rooms, not an unfinished accidental wall. Roofs/floors are distinct stud-connected subassemblies.

## Focal hierarchy
Primary: copper-green observatory dome with a dark viewing slit and gold finial. Supporting: a tall glazed lantern and a recessed arched entrance with sea-blue doors. Secondary: repeated tall windows, restrained pale cornices, harbor steps, planted terrace, map desks, bookshelves and scientific instrument displays.

## Palette
Warm tan limestone dominates; dark tan at the quay and masonry accents; pale ivory/white trim; sand green and dark green copper roof bands; dark blue-gray foundation; reddish-brown furniture; dark frames and transparent glazing; small warm gold/brass highlights. Blue sea and concentrated green planting stay on the low foreground. Large wall and dome surfaces remain quiet.

## Construction intent
Use bonded System bricks/plates, inspected arched windows and dedicated fittings. Allocate real wall openings, stud-supported cornices, floors, roof and terrace before ornament. Rounded roof built only from real unscaled physical parts. No arbitrary polygons, stretched pieces or pasted facade ornaments.

## Modules and build order
1. Bonded sea/quay base and stairs.
2. Main hall ground floor: arcaded front, entrance, side glazing, marine-chart furniture.
3. Main hall upper reading room: windows, books and observation instruments.
4. Dome roof: supporting roof deck, octagonal drum, stepped copper shell and slit.
5. Tower lower, middle and lantern stages with independent roof.
6. Terrace fittings, planters and restrained coastal planting.
