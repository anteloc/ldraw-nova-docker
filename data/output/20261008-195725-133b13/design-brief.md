# Design Brief: The Cozy Cottage

*   **Identity:** A small, charming modern suburban cottage.
*   **Silhouette:** Asymmetrical design. A steep A-frame roof on the main body with a dormer, contrasted by a flat-roofed, slightly recessed entrance extension.
*   **Focal Hierarchy:**
    *   **Primary:** A welcoming front entrance with a porch and a vibrant Reddish-Brown door.
    *   **Supporting:** A dormer window to break the roofline; a modest brick chimney.
*   **Palette Roles:**
    *   **Walls:** White / Light Grey.
    *   **Roof:** Dark Stone Grey.
    *   **Accents:** Reddish-Brown (door, window frames).
    *   **Foundation:** Medium Stone Grey.
*   **Depth:** Recessed windows and a projecting porch canopy to create interesting shadows.
*   **Detail Vocabulary:** Consistent brick masonry, arched windows on the ground floor, simple pane windows upstairs, and a small flower box.
*   **Build Order:** Foundation -> Floor 1 -> Floor 2 -> Roof/Chimney -> Details.
