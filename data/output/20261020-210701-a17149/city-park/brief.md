# Maple Green Park – "Sunny Sunday" (design brief)

**Concept.** A 48x48-stud city park (3x3 green 16x16 baseplates) on a sunny day, with exactly 20 minifigures each doing a different activity.

**Silhouette / layout (cells = studs, x east, z south, centre of the map = plaza):**
- Centre: 12x12 tan plaza with a stone-ringed blue pond and a small white fountain with trans-blue spray; four tan paths run to the baseplate edges.
- NW: playground – sandbox (brown bricks, sand plate), yellow-tower/blue-stepped slide, tilted see-saw.
- NE: white/red gazebo bandstand with a musician; bench beside the east path; cherry-blossom tree.
- SE: red/white picnic blanket (checker plates), BBQ with smoke, sunbather towel, ice-cream cart (red/white awning) on the south path.
- SW: flower bed + hedge with gardener, bench with reader; lamp posts at plaza corners; trees (green, bright green, blossom) around the edges.

**Palette:** green/bright-green grass, tan paths, medium-blue water, light-grey stone, red/white/yellow accents, flower colours (red, yellow, white, pink, orange).

**20 people:** 1 slide-top kid, 2 slide kid, 3 sandbox kid, 4-5 see-saw kids, 6 soccer kid, 7-8 picnic couple, 9 BBQ cook, 10 sunbather, 11 duck-feeder, 12 ice-cream vendor, 13 customer, 14 photographer, 15 jogger, 16-17 elderly pair, 18 banjo musician, 19 bench reader, 20 gardener.

**Build order:** 20 figures via the minifig rig (generate.py calls `minifig plan`), scenery generated with a local-frame helper (rotatable modules), then build/review/minifig check.

**Scope notes / simplifications:** no dog, kite or swing (no suitable parts / unsupported hanging); see-saw tilted 8 degrees as a rigid rotation, its seated kids are therefore unsupported by studs (warning expected).
