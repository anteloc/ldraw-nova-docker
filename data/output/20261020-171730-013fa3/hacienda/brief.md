# Hacienda del Sol - design brief

**Identity:** A colonial Mexican courtyard hacienda (casa de hacienda): ochre walls, white arcade arches, red clay-tile roofs, a white bell tower (campanario) and a tiled courtyard with a fountain.

**Scope:** Large display model on a 64x64-stud baseplate (2x2 of 32x32). Building footprint 52x40 studs, walls 6 bricks high, wings 11 studs deep around a 30x18 courtyard.

**Silhouette:** Low, long ochre ring with red roofs; the white bell tower rises at the front-left (SW) corner and is the tallest mass. A raised portal marks the centre of the front.

**Focal hierarchy:** 1) bell tower + front portal (wooden gate); 2) arched courtyard arcade, fountain, sombrero minifig; 3) windows, plinth, cornice, garden plants, barrels.

**Palette:** walls Bright Light Orange (191); plinth Dark Orange (25); arcade/tower/trim White (15); roof Dark Red (320); wood Reddish Brown (70); gates Black; plants Green/Dark Green with Magenta/Pink flowers; paving Tan/Dark Orange checker in courtyard, Dark Tan base; garden Green.

**Plan (studs; building origin x=6,z=12 on 64x64 base):**
- Each wing is 11 deep: outer wall, 6-deep rooms, inner wall, 2-wide corridor, arcade row.
- Arcade: 1x6 arches (5 along N/S, 3 along E/W) on 1x1 piers, courtyard ring.
- Portal passage: 4 wide through the south wing, aligned with the centre arcade bay; double-bay barred gate and brown door.
- Roof: 3-deep 33-degree slopes at eaves, 45-degree slopes to a ridge row.
- **Open wing:** the north (rear) wing has no roof and a low rear wall as a cutaway; five furnished rooms (bedroom, dining room, kitchen, sala with rug, study). Other rooms stay closed.
- Tower: 6x6, 12 courses, belfry with arches and gold bell, white pyramid roof with a cross finial.
- Courtyard: checker paving, fountain with water, planters, sombrero minifig, papel-picado-style flags (coloured tiles on a line), barrels, cacti-green plants.

**Build order:** base -> paving -> wall courses (generator, greedy brick merging with alternating offsets) -> openings (windows/doors) -> arcade -> roofs -> tower -> furniture -> courtyard -> figure.
