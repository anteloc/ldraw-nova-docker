# Meadow Posy Set – brief

Inspired by LEGO 10280 Flower Bouquet (attached; Orion Pobursky, CCAL 2.0): sand-green axle stems, round-end-plate petal rings
(35480) around a round tile, stacked round bricks for a rose, clipped leaves. No submodel is copied; all arrangements are new.

Five flowers on one planter slab (3027 6x16 plate, tan) with 2x2 round brick sockets (3941, reddish brown) gripping each axle:
1. Daisy – 12 white 35480 petals in two offset tiers, yellow 14769 tile centre. Stem axle 9.
2. Rose – dark-green calyx, stacked red/dark-red round bricks, ring of curved slopes 15068 splayed outward. Axle 12.
3. Tulip – orange cup of 8 curved slopes 15068 standing as walls (2 tiers), yellow core. Axle 8.
4. Lavender – tall axle 16, column of purple 1x1 round plates with cones 4589 sideways, antenna tip.
5. Sunflower – yellow 35480/3069b petal rings, dark-brown seed disc. Axle 12.
Each stem gets a dark-green collar + 2 tilted leaves (2423).

Palette: sand green stems, dark green leaves, white/red/orange/purple/yellow blooms, tan base. Staggered heights make a bouquet silhouette.
Build order: each flower is a submodel (origin = top of axle/head base plate), main places them on the base.
