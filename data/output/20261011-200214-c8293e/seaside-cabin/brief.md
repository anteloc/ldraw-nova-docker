# Brief: Tidewater Cabin (seaside cabin with garden)

## Identity
A quiet holiday cabin on the coast. Warm, weathered, lived-in. One house plus its garden, nothing else.

## Silhouette
- Plinth of 2 studs above sand level (tan), so the floor sits clear of the tide line.
- Ground floor: 8 x 10 studs, weathered wood-brown walls.
- Upper floor: set back one stud on the sea side, with a gable facing the sea.
- Main roof: steep blue-grey, ridge running front to back. A small cupola sits on the ridge.
- Deck wraps the front and one side at floor level, with a porch roof over the door.
- Brick/stone chimney on the side wall.
- Garden fills a ~16 x 20 stud plot: sand path, low white picket fence, two planters, a bench.

## Dimensions (LDU, stud = 20, brick = 24, plate = 8)
- Footprint: 160 x 200 LDU on the ground floor.
- Walls about 48 LDU per storey (two bricks), upper storey set back 20 LDU.

## Palette roles
- Walls: weathered wood-brown (dominant).
- Trim, frames, deck rail: off-white.
- Roof: blue-grey slate, dark trim along eaves.
- Glazing: sea-glass blue, transparent.
- Accent: warm yellow in lit windows and two lanterns.
- Foundation, path, chimney: sand/tan, grey stone for chimney.
- Planting: green, one pink planter as a small accent.

## Focal hierarchy
1. Primary: front door under the porch roof, lanterns on each side.
2. Supporting: round porthole window in the sea-facing gable, and the cupola.
3. Accents: planters, a buoy on the deck, bench, picket fence.

## Depth
- Recessed window openings, sills projecting by one plate.
- Porch roof overhang over the deck.
- Upper storey set back to create a shadow line.

## Build order
1. Plinth and floor plates; check levels and clear openings.
2. Ground-floor walls with door and window openings.
3. Upper floor, gable and porthole.
4. Roof, cupola and chimney.
5. Deck, porch roof, rails, lanterns.
6. Garden: path, fence, planters, bench, buoy.
7. Full review, then a visual revision pass.
